About the Workshop

The IEEE CSS Technical Committee on Energy Systems (TC-ES) is organizing a full-day technical workshop that brings together researchers and industry practitioners who work on control and optimization for modern energy systems.

A university campus in Hawaiʻi is a fitting setting for this workshop, because the islands operate some of the most renewable-rich, inverter-dominated grids in the world. Many of the problems that engineers in Hawaiʻi are solving today inform global solutions for grid-forming control, distributed flexibility, large new loads, and reliability under deep uncertainty.

Program

The program spans the following topics, alongside operational perspectives from Hawaiian Electric.

  • Grid-forming and inverter-based resource control
  • Next-generation grid codes and ancillary services
  • Grid-aware demand response and DER coordination
  • Congestion and voltage management
  • Virtual power plants and market participation
  • Data centers and AI compute as flexible grid assets

Site Tour

The day starts with a tour of the Kapolei Energy Storage facility, a 185 MW / 565 MWh grid-forming battery plant on the western side of Oʻahu that took over grid services from the nearby AES coal plant after the coal plant closed in 2022. Buses will take participants from UH Mānoa to Kapolei in the morning and return them to campus for the afternoon talks.

Logistics

  • The University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa is a short drive (about 15 minutes) or a long walk (about 50 minutes) from the Hilton Hawaiian Village Waikiki Beach Resort, where CDC is being held.
  • The workshop is a separate event from CDC.
  • There is no registration fee.
